# Energy Operations Analytics Platform

An end-to-end analytics engineering project for monitoring energy use, utility costs, billing quality , and operational performance across a portfolio of public facilties.

## Business Problem

Organizations managing many facilties often recieve energy information from disconnected utility accounts, meters, invoices, and building systems. This makes it difficult to identify unusually high consumption, missing billing periods, duplicates records,cost increase and inefficient facilities.This project builds a centralized analytics platform that transforms raw utility data into reliable business metrics and decision-ready reporting.

## 

- Combine facility, utility-account, billing, meter and weather data
- Build repeatable Python and SQL trasformations pipelines
- Detect duplicate records, missing billing periods, and abnormal consumption
- Compare energy performance across facilities and time periods
- Track electricity use, natural gas use, cost, and  estimated emissions
- Produce an executive dashboard with operational KPIs
- Document the data model, assumptions, and validation rules
